# 🌾 Farmer-Centric Crop Price Prediction & Risk Management System

An AI-powered decision support platform designed to **help farmers reduce market risk, plan sales, and maximize profit** using price forecasting, inventory insights, and market intelligence.

This project shifts the traditional *trader-focused* tools into a **farmer-first perspective**, acknowledging that farmers **must sell their produce** and therefore need **timing, quantity, and risk guidance**, not speculation.

---

## 🚜 Problem Statement

Farmers face several challenges:
- They must sell crops regardless of market volatility
- Lack of price visibility for upcoming months
- No guidance on **how much to sell, when to sell**
- High dependency on middlemen and traders
- Poor inventory and risk planning

Most existing tools are **trader-oriented**, focusing on profit maximization rather than **farmer survival and stability**.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

To empower farmers with:
- **Short-term and mid-term crop price predictions**
- **Risk-aware selling strategies**
- **Inventory management insights**
- **Data-driven cultivation & selling decisions**

The goal is **not speculation**, but **risk reduction and income stability**.

---

## 🧠 Core Features

### 📈 Crop Price Prediction
- Predicts crop prices for:
  - **Next 1 month**
  - **Next 3 months**
- Uses historical price data and ML models
- Location-aware (state / city based)

---

### 📦 Inventory Management
- Tracks expected yield vs stored quantity
- Suggests:
  - How much to sell now
  - How much to hold
- Prevents distress selling

---

### ⚖️ Risk Reduction Engine
- Detects:
  - Price volatility
  - Market uncertainty
- Provides safer selling recommendations
- Helps farmers avoid selling at lowest prices

---

### 🌱 Farmer-Centric Decision Logic
Unlike traders:
- Farmers **will cultivate anyway**
- Farmers **must sell produce**
- System focuses on:
  - Optimal selling windows
  - Quantity-based selling strategies
  - Cash-flow stability
